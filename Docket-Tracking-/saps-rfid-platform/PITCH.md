# RFID Spatial Intelligence Platform
## Funding Pitch Summary

### The Problem

South African organizations lose millions annually to misplaced, stolen, or mismanaged inventory. Current solutions show data in spreadsheets. When something goes missing, staff spend hours manually searching.

**Real examples:**
- SAPS evidence lockers: 30+ days to locate misplaced forensic evidence
- Hospital equipment: R50k items "lost" in the building
- Mine sites: Safety equipment missing during inspections
- Retail: Shrinkage exceeds 2% of revenue

### Our Solution

A 3D digital twin of your facility where you can SEE where everything is. Not tables. Not lists. An actual visual representation of your space with every tagged item shown in its real location.

**Search anything. Find it in seconds. Watch the camera fly to it.**

### What Makes This Different

| Traditional RFID | Our Platform |
|------------------|--------------|
| Tables and spreadsheets | 3D visualization |
| Manual zone checks | AI anomaly detection |
| Reactive alerts | Predictive warnings |
| One-size-fits-all | Industry-specific rules |

### Innovation

1. **3D Digital Twin** - First in SA market. Items appear where they physically are.

2. **AI Pattern Detection** - System learns normal behavior and flags anomalies:
   - Item stuck too long in one place
   - Unauthorized zone exit
   - Unusual movement sequences
   - After-hours activity

3. **Multi-Tenant SaaS** - One platform serves multiple industries with custom terminology and rules.

4. **Search-First UX** - Type what you need, click result, camera flies to location.
