# South African RFID Tracking Solutions Market Research Report
## Executive Summary

The South African market for RFID tracking solutions presents significant opportunities across government and private sectors, with a total addressable market exceeding R15 billion annually. The SAPS forensic laboratories represent a high-priority entry point with dedicated technology budgets of R7+ billion, while the private security industry (valued at R45+ billion annually) offers substantial scaling potential.

## Market Analysis

### 1. SAPS & Government Market

#### Budget Allocation & Technology Spending
- **Total SAPS Budget 2025/26**: R120.89 billion
- **SITA Technology Budget**: R7 billion (2024/25)
- **DCDT Digital Budget**: R7.8 billion (2025/26)
- **Total Government IT Spending**: R12.2+ billion over medium-term

#### Forensic Laboratory Priorities
- **Performance Target Increase**: SAPS raised forensic report targets from 25,000 to 100,000 for 2025/26
- **Infrastructure Investment**: Major expansion of forensic laboratories with state-of-the-art technology
- **Equipment Modernization**: 450 body-worn cameras, 200 dashboard cameras, 17 surveillance drones procured
- **Backlog Reduction**: Critical focus on reducing DNA and evidence backlogs

#### Government Procurement Requirements
- **Tender Portal**: eTenders.gov.za for all opportunities
- **Mandatory Requirements**:
  - Central Supplier Database (CSD) registration
  - Valid SARS Tax Clearance Certificate
  - B-BBEE certification (ICT Sector Code applies)
  - Standard Bidding Documents (SBDs 1, 6.1, 6.2, 8, 9)
- **Evaluation System**: 80/20 or 90/10 preference points (price vs B-BBEE)
- **Functionality Threshold**: Must meet minimum technical requirements
