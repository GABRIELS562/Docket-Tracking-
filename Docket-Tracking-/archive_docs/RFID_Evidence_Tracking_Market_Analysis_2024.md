## 8. Risk Assessment & Mitigation

### Market Risks

**Technology Adoption Resistance**
- Risk: Slow adoption in conservative law enforcement
- Mitigation: Pilot programs, strong ROI demonstration, reference customers

**Budget Constraints**
- Risk: Limited technology budgets in government agencies
- Mitigation: Flexible pricing, grant funding assistance, phased implementation

**Competitive Response**
- Risk: Established players adding 3D capabilities
- Mitigation: Patent protection, rapid feature development, customer lock-in

### Technical Risks

**Scalability Challenges**
- Risk: Performance issues with large evidence volumes
- Mitigation: Cloud-native architecture, performance optimization, load testing

**Integration Complexity**
- Risk: Difficulty integrating with existing systems
- Mitigation: API-first design, professional services, partner ecosystem

### Regulatory Risks

**Compliance Requirements**
- Risk: Changing evidence management regulations
- Mitigation: Close monitoring, compliance expertise, flexible architecture

**Data Security Concerns**
- Risk: Security breaches in sensitive forensic data
- Mitigation: SOC 2 compliance, encryption, regular audits
