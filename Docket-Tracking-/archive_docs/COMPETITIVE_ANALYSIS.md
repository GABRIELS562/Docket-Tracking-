### 1. INFOR WMS (www.infor.com)

**Product:** Infor Warehouse Management System

**What They Offer:**
- ✅ RFID integration
- ✅ Real-time tracking
- ✅ **FULL 3D visualization** (3D warehouse layouts)
- ✅ Interactive 3D models
- ✅ Digital twin technology

**Their 3D Capabilities:**
- Real-time 3D warehouse visualization
- Interactive facility layouts
- Item location rendering in 3D space
- Advanced spatial analytics

**Pricing:**
- 💰 **$100,000 - $150,000+ per year**
- 💰 R1.85M - R2.78M+ annually (at R18.50/$)

- Requires extensive implementation (6-12 months)

**Market Position:** Top-tier enterprise WMS for large corporations

**Limitations:**
- ❌ Completely unaffordable for mid-market
- ❌ Requires dedicated IT team
- ❌ Long implementation timeline
- ❌ Not suitable for government departments with limited budgets

### 2. MANHATTAN ACTIVE WMS (www.manh.com)

**Product:** Manhattan Active Warehouse Management

**What They Offer:**
- ✅ RFID tracking
- ✅ Real-time visibility
- ✅ **3D facility visualization** (digital twin)
- ✅ Advanced analytics
- ✅ Cloud-native platform

**Their 3D Capabilities:**
- Digital twin warehouse models
- 3D layout planning
- Real-time 3D tracking visualization
- Spatial optimization

**Pricing:**
- 💰 **$80,000 - $150,000+ per year**
- 💰 R1.48M - R2.78M+ annually
- SaaS model with per-warehouse pricing
- Implementation: 4-9 months

**Market Position:** Leading cloud WMS for large enterprises

**Limitations:**
- ❌ Enterprise-only pricing
- ❌ Requires substantial training
- ❌ Built for massive operations
- ❌ Not scalable for smaller facilities

### 3. ZEBRA MOTIONWORKS (www.zebra.com)

**Product:** Zebra MotionWorks Enterprise

**What They Offer:**
- ✅ RFID tracking (Zebra hardware)
- ✅ Real-time location systems (RTLS)
- ✅ **Partial 3D visualization** (mostly 2D with some 3D views)
- ✅ Indoor positioning

**Their 3D Capabilities:**
- Basic 3D floor plan views
- Primarily 2D maps with layers

- Focus on positioning accuracy over visualization

**Pricing:**
- 💰 **$50,000 - $100,000+ per year**
- 💰 R925k - R1.85M+ annually
- Hardware + software bundle
- Implementation: 3-6 months

**Market Position:** Enterprise asset tracking and RTLS

**Limitations:**
- ❌ 3D visualization is basic (not immersive)
- ❌ Primarily 2D focus
- ❌ Requires Zebra hardware ecosystem
- ❌ Still enterprise pricing
