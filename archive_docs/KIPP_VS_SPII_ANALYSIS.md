```

Stage: Commercialization and market penetration

Target: Township & rural entrepreneurs (grassroots focus)
```

**Key Quote:**
> "Only locally developed innovations, both products or services, processes and technology that are **already fully developed and market-ready**, will be considered under the KIPP."

**Translation:** They want products READY TO SELL NOW, not pilot deployments.

```
Purpose: Industrial innovation funding (R&D to pre-production)

1. **Product Process Development (PPD)** - For pre-production pilots ✅ **YOUR FIT**

### **3. FUNDING TYPE & AMOUNTS**

#### **KIPP Funding:**
```
Maximum: R7 million per transaction
Type:
  - Limited grants (small portion)
  - Equity (they take ownership stake)
  - Quasi-equity
  - Subordinated loans
  - Hybrid instruments

Pricing: "Capital preservation requirements and cost recoveries"
Timeline: Medium to long-term
Security: Little to no security requirements
```

**Translation:** They prefer LOANS or EQUITY (you pay back or give ownership).

**Grant Component:** Small, not primary funding mechanism.

---

#### **SPII Funding:**
```
Maximum: Typically R2M-R5M+ (can go higher)
Type:
  - GRANTS (50-85% of qualifying costs, NO PAYBACK!)
  - You provide matching funds (15-50%)

Grant % Based on BEE Ownership:
  >50% BEE: 85% grant (PPD) or 75% grant (Matching)
  25-50% BEE: 75% grant (PPD) or 65% grant (Matching)
  <25% BEE: 50% grant (PPD) or 50% grant (Matching)

Timeline: 12-36 months typical
Security: NOT applicable (grants don't require security)
```

**Translation:** FREE MONEY (grants) that you DON'T pay back!

**Example for your R2.01M request:**
```

- SPII PPD Grant: R1.71M (85%)
